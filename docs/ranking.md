# Ranking

Ranking is a recommendation, not a decision. All useful results remain
selectable in MPV selection mode.

The current scorer considers language, exact media hash, year, resolution,
source, TV season/episode, hearing-impaired preference, and forced status.
Scores are normalized to a maximum of 100.

Scores are intentionally heuristic. Users can open the menu, choose another
result, cancel, or correct the media identity and search again.
